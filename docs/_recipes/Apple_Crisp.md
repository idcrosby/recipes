## Ingredients

- 6 apples, sliced thin
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- ¾ cup flour
- ¾ cup rolled oats
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup soft margarine
