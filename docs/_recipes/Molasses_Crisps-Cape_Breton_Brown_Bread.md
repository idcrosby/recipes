## Ingredients

### Molasses crisps

- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- ¼ tsp ginger
- ½ cup shortening
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup molasses
- ½ cup rolled oats
{: .ingredients }

### Cape Breton brown bread

- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 cup rolled oats
- ½ cup molasses
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp butter
- Yeast dissolved in ½ cup water
- 5 cups flour
{: .ingredients }
