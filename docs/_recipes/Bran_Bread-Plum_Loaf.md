## Ingredients

### Bran bread

- 1 cup chopped dates
- 1 cup All-Bran
- ½ cup molasses
- 2 tbsp shortening
- ¾ cup hot water
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup flour
- 1½ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
{: .ingredients }

### Plum loaf

- 12 cups flour
- 2 tbsp salt
- ½ cup shortening
- ½ cup molasses
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 lb raisins, washed and floured
- ½ tsp allspice
- Dissolved yeast
- Lukewarm water
{: .ingredients }
