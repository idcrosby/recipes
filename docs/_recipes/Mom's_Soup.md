## Ingredients

- ½ cup barley
- 8 cups water
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp gravy or Oxo cube
- Roast bones or leftover meat, cut small
- Salt and pepper
- 1 large carrot, cut small
- 1 large potato, cut small
- Turnip, cut small
- Celery tops (optional)
- 1 can tomato soup
