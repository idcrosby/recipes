### Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- ½ tsp allspice
- 2 packages rapid-mix yeast (or regular yeast mixed in water)
- ¾ cup milk
- ½ cup water
- ¼ cup butter
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 cup raisins or currants
