## Ingredients

- 375 mL raisins
- Boiling water to cover the raisins
- 125 mL margarine
- 175 mL brown sugar
- 125 mL molasses
- 4 eggs
- 500 mL milk
- 500 mL bran
- 250 mL whole wheat flour
- 500 mL white flour
- 15 mL baking powder
- 5 mL baking soda
- 5 mL salt
